Week 8 Announcements

Oct 12
  • This is the last week we holding DUEs for Project 1. Please refer to #167 for more information.
    • Please try to sign up for one of the open slots if you can. If signups are full, or you cannot find any slot that works for you, or you need to request a DUE past October 14 for some other reason, please make a private question on Ed.
    • To check your DUEs score, you can submit any file (as many times as you wish) to the [Ungraded] DUEs Completion Checker assignment in Gradescope.
  • Congrats on finishing the midterm. We have released the solutions (#165).
    • Regrades will close this Wednesday October 14th at 11:59pm. Please make sure to submit all of your regrade requests before then as we will not be accepting any regrades past this date.
    • If your regrade involves changing the rubric itself, you must post in the relevant question Ed thread rather than make a regrade request.
  • To clear up a bit of confusion over attendance, feel free to read more about it here https://fa26.cs161.org/policies/#attendance, but attendance points captured through PollEV participation during lectures can be used to gain points back on the exams. We count both in person and zoom attendance.
    • We will also be allowing 2 missed attendances to be dropped (not counted in participation_percentage).
